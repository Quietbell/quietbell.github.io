How to choose

  • Noise control: can you choose events, projects, issue types, priorities and status changes per space? Is there JQL?
  • Readability: are updates for one issue grouped in a thread?
  • Visibility of failures: when a message doesn't arrive, does the app tell you why?
  • Who can configure it: only Jira admins, or project admins too?
  • Data: where does the app run, and which servers receive your issue data?
  • Price at your size: compare at your real number of users, not at the first tier.

What Threadbell does

Rules per Google Chat space with event, project, type, priority, status and JQL filters; one thread per issue; daily digests; personal alerts for people who are assigned or mentioned; a delivery log with plain-language errors and automatic retries; a page for project admins. It runs on Atlassian Forge and only contacts the Google Chat API.
