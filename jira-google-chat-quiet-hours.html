Quiet hours for Jira notifications in Google Chat

Updated 20 November 2026 · 4 min read

A Jira space in Google Chat is useful between 9 and 18. At 23:40 it is a phone buzzing because a colleague in another time zone closed six tickets. People solve this by muting the space, and then they miss the one message that mattered. Quiet hours solve it properly.

What quiet hours should do

  • Hold, not drop: updates made during the night are kept.
  • One summary when the quiet ends: "While you were away · 4 issues", with the number of updates per issue and a link.
  • Exceptions stay live: a new Highest-priority bug should still ring.
  • Per space, in the space's time zone: the Lisbon team and the Toronto team do not share a night.

Why muting in Google Chat is not enough

Muting a space silences everything, including @all and the critical alerts. It also hides the backlog of messages, so the next morning starts with a scroll through 40 cards. A digest of the night is faster to read and nothing is lost.

Grouped updates: the other half of the noise

Quiet hours fix the night. Bursts fix the day: a release that transitions 30 issues in two minutes, a bulk edit of labels, a bot that comments on every build. The answer is grouping: when several updates hit the same issue within a short window, send one message with the list of changes instead of one message per change. Keep it short (2 to 5 minutes), so live notifications still feel live.

Setting it up in Threadbell

  1. Open the rule of the space and the Quiet hours section.
  2. Choose from/to and the time zone, for example 20:00 to 08:00, Europe/Paris.
  3. Mode Summary (one "While you were away" message at the end) or Silence (nothing kept) for spaces that are pure noise at night.
  4. Optional: Group updates on the same issue within 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  5. Keep a separate rule with no quiet hours for Highest priority only, posted with @all.

Quiet hours apply to the space, not to people. For personal alerts ("assigned to me", "mentioned"), each person sets their own in My Google Chat alerts.

Doing it with Jira Automation instead

Possible, but heavy: a condition on {{now.hour}} can skip the web request at night, and a second scheduled rule can post a morning search. You lose the per-issue counts and the grouping, and you maintain two rules per space. Fine for one space; not for ten.
