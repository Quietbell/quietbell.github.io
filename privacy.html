Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what the Threadbell app for Jira ("the app"), published by Quietbell ("we"), does with data.

What the app stores

The app stores the following in Atlassian Forge storage, inside Atlassian's infrastructure and separately for each Jira site:

  • Your notification rules: name, events, filters and message options.
  • The Google Chat webhook URL of each rule, kept as an encrypted secret.
  • A delivery log of up to 150 entries: time, rule, issue key, event type, result and error reason.
  • A count of messages sent per month.

The app does not store issue content, comments, user names, email addresses or account IDs. We run no servers of our own and have no access to data stored in your site's Forge storage.

What the app sends, and where

When an issue event matches one of your rules, the app reads the issue from Jira and sends a message to the Google Chat webhook you configured, at chat.googleapis.com. Depending on the rule's settings, the message can contain the issue key, summary, type, project name, status, priority, the display names of the assignee and of the person who made the change, and excerpts of the comment and description. Google processes that message under your organisation's Google Workspace agreement.

The app sends data nowhere else. It contains no analytics and no tracking.

Data we receive from Atlassian

As a Marketplace partner we receive from Atlassian the licensing and billing contact details of customers who install the app. We use them only for support, billing questions and important notices about the app.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a rule deletes its webhook URL. Uninstalling the app deletes all data the app stored for your site, under Atlassian's Forge data retention rules. The delivery log keeps only the latest 150 entries.

Support requests

If you email us, we keep your message for as long as needed to answer and to follow up, then delete it within 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, such as support emails. Write to quietbell.apps@proton.me.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
